The problem

Small and medium creators get
ignored. By brands and by other creators.

01Brands won't reach out.

Below 10k followers, agencies don't know you exist. You're invisible to the people writing the cheques.

02Cold DMs go nowhere.

You message bigger creators hoping for a collab. They never reply. Your message sits in "Requests" forever.

03The algorithm rewards collabs.

Cross-posting and shared Reels are the fastest way to grow on Instagram in 2026. But only if you can find a partner.

04You don't know who's actually real.

Half the "creators" you see have bought followers. You waste time on collabs that go nowhere.
